“How does trust shape the way people interpret, rely on, and interact with AI?”

I study how people form trust, make judgments, verify information, and disclose personal experiences in technology-mediated contexts, with a growing focus on Human-AI Interaction and Human-AI reliance.

Current Research Question

From AI-assisted work to questions about verification

My current professional use of AI-assisted automation systems has made trust visible as a practical and researchable problem: when a system produces useful outputs, people may become faster, more confident, and more dependent on it. I am interested in studying how users decide when to verify AI-generated work, when they accept it, and how repeated use shapes reliance over time.
