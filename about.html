About

Emerging interdisciplinary researcher in Human-AI Interaction

I am developing a research agenda around Human-AI Interaction, trust, reliance, judgment, and self-disclosure. My background connects behavioral research, mixed methods, consumer research, quantitative fieldwork, and applied problem solving.

Research Identity

My central question is how trust shapes the way people interpret, rely on, and interact with AI. I am especially interested in how repeated interaction, perceived familiarity, and emotionally sensitive contexts influence verification behavior, confidence, and self-disclosure.

Current Direction

My current work with AI-assisted automation systems gives me a practical lens on trust and reliance. It raises research questions about when people check AI output, when they accept it, and how system usefulness may change behavior over time.

Across these projects, my focus gradually shifted from analyzing information itself to understanding how people interpret, trust, and act upon information.
